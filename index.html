Liu Luo

Associate Research Professor · Chair of the Department of Surveying and Geographic Information

Liu Luo is an Associate Research Professor and Chair of the Department of Surveying and Geographic Information, College of Natural Resources and Environment, South China Agricultural University. He received his Ph.D. in Cartography and Geographic Information System from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences and visited the University of Oklahoma as a government-sponsored scholar from 2018 to 2019. His research uses geographic information systems and remote sensing to address questions in global climate change, ecological environment, agroecology, and land-use/land-cover change.

Research

Research Interests

His work integrates remote sensing observations, geographic information systems, crop models, and machine learning to support agricultural resource monitoring, food production analysis, and ecological-environmental assessment of land-use/land-cover change.

  • Agricultural remote sensing: cropping intensity, cropland abandonment, parcel extraction, and multi-source mapping.
  • Crop modeling: food production potential, agricultural land-use transition, and scenario assessment.
  • Ecological and environmental effects assessment of land-use/land-cover change, including water-energy-food nexus and cultivated land multifunctionality.

Research

研究方向

研究工作结合遥感观测、地理信息系统、作物模型和机器学习方法，支撑农业资源监测、粮食生产分析与 土地利用/覆被变化生态环境效应评估。

  • 农业遥感监测：作物种植强度、耕地撂荒、农田地块提取与多源遥感制图。
  • 作物模型模拟：面向粮食生产潜力、农业土地利用转型和情景评估的模型分析。
  • 土地利用/覆被变化生态环境效应评估，例如水-能-粮关系、耕地多功能等。
